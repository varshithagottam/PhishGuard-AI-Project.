    STAY INFORMED

    Cyber Awareness

    Learn how to recognise common message-based scams.

    ⚠

    Urgent account warnings

    Scammers may claim your bank account or SIM will be blocked unless you act immediately.

    Safety tip

    Open the official app or contact the organisation using a verified number.

    ↗

    Suspicious links

    Fake websites can imitate banks, delivery services, or popular brands to steal information.

    Safety tip

    Check the complete domain. Do not trust a link just because it contains a familiar brand name.

    ₹

    UPI and payment scams

    Fraudsters may ask you to scan a QR code, approve a collect request, or share a PIN.

    Safety tip

    You do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

    ♙

    OTP and identity theft

    Someone pretending to be support staff may ask for an OTP, password, PIN, or CVV.

    Safety tip

    Never share these details with anyone, including callers claiming to be bank staff.

    In India: If you experience financial cyber fraud, contact your bank immediately and report it through the national cybercrime helpline 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
